Mirra AI Support

We’re happy to help with the Mirra AI app and Mirra AI in ChatGPT.

Email: support@askmirra.ai. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Common questions

  • I can’t sign in from ChatGPT. Use the same email and password (or Google / Apple account) as in the Mirra app. New to Mirra? Create your account in the app first: Google Play · App Store.
  • How do I disconnect Mirra from ChatGPT? In ChatGPT: Settings → Apps / Plugins → Mirra AI → Disconnect.
  • How do I import my Myntra, Amazon, Savana, AJIO or Nykaa Fashion orders? In the Mirra app: Wardrobe → Import from Store.
  • How do I delete my account and data? In the app: Profile → Delete Account, or follow askmirra.ai/delete-account.html.
  • Does Mirra sell jewellery, bags or beauty products? Not yet. Mirra covers clothing and footwear.
